# Bobiverse companion data: books 1–2 only

Scope: *We Are Legion (We Are Bob)* (2016) and *For We Are Many* (2017). Anything first revealed in *All These Worlds* or later is deliberately excluded. Generated by `/workspace/raw/build.py` and checked by `/workspace/raw/validate.py`, both on the box.

## Conventions
- Ids are kebab-case. The root is `bob` (`isRoot: true`, `parentId: null`). The human Robert Johansson is in `characters.json` as `robert-johansson`.
- Generation numbering: replicant Bob = 1, his clones = 2, their clones = 3, and so on.
- `parentKnown: false` together with `parentId: null` and `generation: null` means books 1–2 sources don't establish the parent. These Bobs are not guesses; leave them out of the tree or draw them in an "unknown lineage" bucket.
- `parentSource`: `author-genealogy` comes from Dennis E. Taylor's own chart (http://dennisetaylor.org/2017/04/07/genealogy-of-the-bobs/, image Genealogy-3.png, published April 2017 after book 2). `wiki-riker-page` comes from the fan wiki only. The chart agrees with Kurt Anderson's "Bob-by-construction" chart (http://www.kurt-anderson.com/main/uploads/2017/04/Bob-by-construction.pdf).
- Bob `systems` lists systems tied to the Bob in his first book. `book2Systems` lists systems added in book 2 for a Bob who first appears in book 1. `bobsVisited` on systems includes stated destinations (Marvin → Pi³ Orionis) but not systems a Bob only watches remotely. Gamma Leporis has an empty `bobsVisited` because Bender's arrival isn't confirmed in books 1–2.
- `notable` is a list of `{book, text}`. `book2Notes` holds facts that change or appear in book 2 for book-1 entities.
- Events come from the fan-wiki Timeline (rows tagged as books 1–2), sorted by in-universe year, month, book and chapter (`order`). `firstBook` and `chapter` are the book and chapter where the event happens. Event descriptions are close to the wiki wording. `bobsInvolved` is matched automatically from the point-of-view Bob plus Bobs named in the row, so it includes Bobs who are only mentioned. Three backstory rows (2036, 2040, 2041) have no chapter and are tagged book 1.
- Star astrometry: RA and Dec are ICRS/J2000 in degrees. `distanceLy` = 3261.5638 / parallax(mas), using the parallax in each star's Wikipedia infobox (reference in `astrometryRef`; mostly Gaia DR3). `galacticXYZly` is heliocentric galactic Cartesian in light-years (x toward the Galactic Centre, y toward rotation, z toward the north galactic pole), computed from the Hipparcos ICRS→galactic matrix. Sol is at the origin. Distances can differ slightly from the fan wiki, which uses older Hipparcos or RECONS values (for example Epsilon Eridani 10.47 here vs 10.52 on the wiki).

## Uncertain / unresolved
- **Pete**: the timeline says Bob's "two new clones" are Pete and Victor (book 2 ch. 24), but the author's genealogy lists only Victor under Bob. Parent left unknown.
- **Sam** (Exodus-3) and **Monty**: the fan wiki gives Riker as parent; neither is in the author chart. Linked to Riker with `confidence: uncertain`.
- **Marcus, Jeffrey (timeline spelling "Jeffery"), Milton, Zeke, Verne, Surly, Hank**: named in Loki's second strike or at Poseidon; parents unknown.
- **Elmer**: in Khan's first strike (book 1 ch. 60, where the timeline says Khan was the only survivor) and again in the second strike (book 2 ch. 50). Unresolved.
- **First-strike fates**: deaths of the individual first-strike Bobs aren't recorded per Bob.
- **Hal**: self-destructs at GL 877 (May 2188) yet scouts it again in Apr 2196. Probably a restored backup; not stated.
- **Thor**: parent Calvin per the author chart; home system is "probably Alpha Centauri" per the wiki.
- **Kappa Ceti**: the books don't say which star. The coordinates are for κ¹ Ceti (the wiki's guess).
- **Gamma Leporis**: Bender's destination only.
- **HIP 84051**: no Wikipedia article. Astrometry from Wikidata Q86704518 (Gaia DR2), 41.6 ly; the wiki says 40.7 ly (Hipparcos).
- **NN 4285**: no confirmed catalog match (GJ 4285 is a different star about 92 ly away). The fan wiki claims about 22.2 ly (CNS3), unverified. Distance and coordinates are null; don't plot it.
- **Hazjiar / the Pav choosing HIP 84051**: the timeline puts it in book 2 ch. 77; the character page and the Major Characters table say book 3. Kept as book 2 with `uncertain`. The timeline says Bill wakes her; her page says Jacques.
- **Matias Araujo**: the Brazilian probes at Sol are book 1, but whether he is named in book 1 isn't confirmed.
- **Cruella**: the wiki says books 1–2; chapter not confirmed.
- **The Others**: their aftermath is seen in book 1 (Beta Hydri); whether the name "the Others" is used in book 1 isn't confirmed. Home system GL 877 is a book 2 reveal.
- **Hippogriffs**: book 1 ch. 53 mentions a "new predator", but the hippogriff name and identification come in book 2.

## Source conflicts noted (wiki vs wiki)
- Quilt page: Edwin escorted Exodus-7. Timeline: Rudy and Edwin came on Exodus-6, and Dexter escorted Exodus-7 to Vulcan. The timeline is used.
- The Bridget page names HIP 84051 as Howard's destination. The timeline says HIP 14101. The timeline is used.
- The Phineas page tags his first appearance as book 3, but the timeline (book 2 ch. 73) and the author's 2017 chart support book 2.
- The Bridget row in the Major Characters table marks book 1; the timeline introduces her in book 2 ch. 25. Book 2 is used.
- The Gliese 877 page dates Bashful's arrival to Nov 2177; the timeline says Nov 2187. The timeline is used.
- Timeline typos: "85 Eridani" (book 2 ch. 34) should be 82 Eridani; "GL 577" (book 2 ch. 59) is probably GL 877.
- Book 1 ch. 60 calls Kyle "Jackson" and Ned "Hector", an acknowledged naming error. Both are recorded as aliases.
- The wiki's List of Bobs puts Milo in generation 1; the author chart puts him under Bob.

## Deliberately excluded (book 3+ or unclear)
- Bobs: Bruce (book uncertain), and Isaac, Jack, Owen and all later Bobs.
- Bender's actual fate and route.
- The 82 Eridani planet names (Asgard, Valhalla, Takamagahara). Book 2 says only "two habitable planets and a habitable moon".
- The names "Quilt" (KKP), "Newholme" (Gamma Pavonis 1) and "Odin" (HIP 14101 Jovian).
- Eta Leporis.
- Species: Dragons, Leviathans and the Quinlans.
- Archimedes' death and anything after Apr 2221.

- Thor kept (author genealogy; absent from the reference map).
- Parent links where our NOTES cite author genealogy / wiki conflicts (e.g. Claude→Mario,
  Dexter→Charles, Sam→Riker uncertain) — reference differs on some of these; we did not
  overwrite site `bobs.json` parents solely from the map.

  placeholders in the reference).
